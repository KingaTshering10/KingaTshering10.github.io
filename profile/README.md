### About

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate of North South University, where I finished
*summa cum laude* with a CGPA of **3.90/4.00** as a recipient of **His Majesty's Scholarship**.

I build and evaluate machine learning systems for vision, language, and medical imaging. My work
is motivated by a single question: **what makes a model trustworthy enough to deploy?** That has
taken me through benchmarking geo-temporal reasoning in vision–language models, decoding sparse
subnetworks in hyperspectral classification, few-shot open-set recognition for skin lesions, and
explainable ML for pediatric health screening.

I am applying to graduate programs in machine learning and am open to research collaborations.
