---

A single accuracy number hides more than it reports. A model that scores well may have answered a
different question than the one it was asked; a network that survives pruning tells you something
about which parameters ever mattered. **I work on measuring what a model actually does** — and I am
drawn to problems where getting the measurement right _is_ the contribution.

Currently finishing my B.Sc. at North South University, with two papers accepted at ICML and EMNLP 2026. **Applying to Ph.D. programs in machine learning, and open to research collaborations.**

## Research interests

| Area                               | The question underneath it                                                                             |
| :--------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **LLM evaluation and reliability** | Does a benchmark score measure reasoning, or format-following? Separating compliance from correctness. |
| **Efficient deep learning**        | Under pruning, _which_ parameters survive — and what does that reveal about what the model learned?    |
| **Interpretability**               | What do learned representations encode, once you strip away the parts the task never needed?           |
