### Research Interests

**Evaluation and reliability of large language models.** **Efficient and resource-aware deep
learning**, including network pruning and model compression. **Interpretability of learned
representations.**

I care about the gap between a benchmark number and a model you can actually rely on — whether
that means separating instruction-following compliance from answer correctness, or asking which
parameters a pruned network genuinely needed.

I am applying to graduate programs in machine learning and am open to research collaborations.
